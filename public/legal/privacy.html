HabitGlo Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HabitGlo collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website, browser demo, and desktop app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect account information (such as email address), usage data related to app functionality, subscription status, and customer support communications.

2. How We Use Information

We use data to provide and maintain HabitGlo, authenticate users, synchronize settings and habits, process subscriptions, improve product quality, and support users.

3. Payment Processing

Payments are processed by Paddle as merchant of record. We do not store full payment card information. Paddle may collect billing and transaction details according to its own privacy policy.

4. Service Providers

We use third-party providers to operate HabitGlo, including infrastructure, authentication, analytics, and payment services. These providers process data only as needed to deliver their services.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

6. Your Choices

You may request updates, export, or deletion of your account data by contacting support. Some data may be retained where legally required.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

HabitGlo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates are posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@habitglo.com
