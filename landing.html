Still not sure HabitGlo is right for you?

Will this actually help me follow through?
HabitGlo works because it removes reliance on memory and motivation. Your commitments stay visible while you work, so you get more reps of the behavior you actually want.
Will this distract me while I work?
No. Dock the strip to the top or bottom and reserve screen space so your apps keep the main area. Tune colors and glow so the LED stays peripheral while you work.
How fast can I start?
In a couple of minutes. Install, choose a protocol, and your reminders are live. You can refine style and behavior settings as you go.
Is my data private?
Yes. Your habits stay private by default. Leaderboard visibility is opt-in, and you control what is shared.
Can I test it before buying?
Yes. Use the browser demo to feel how the reminder layer works before checkout. Desktop app support is currently Windows 10+.
