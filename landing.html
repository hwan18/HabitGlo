    Will this actually help me follow through?

    HabitGlo works because it removes reliance on memory and motivation. Your commitments stay visible while you work, so you get more reps of the behavior you actually want.

    Will this distract me while I work?

    No. Dock the strip to the top or bottom and reserve screen space so your apps keep the main area. Pick a calm color and a slower speed so the LED stays peripheral while you work.

    How fast can I start?

    In a couple of minutes. Install it and five starter reminders are already scrolling. Swap in your own whenever you like.

    Is my data private?

    Yes. Your reminders are saved on your PC and synced only to your own account. Nothing is shared.

    Can I test it before buying?

    Yes. Use the browser demo to feel how the reminder layer works before checkout. Desktop app support is currently Windows 10+.

    Can I cancel monthly anytime?

    Yes. Cancel any time and keep access through the end of the current billing period.
