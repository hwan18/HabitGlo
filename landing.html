FAQ

Common questions.

What exactly is HabitGlo?
HabitGlo is a desktop app that puts a sleek LED-style scrolling ticker on your screen. Your habits and reminders scroll continuously in your peripheral vision. The overlay is always-on-top but supports click-through mode so it never blocks your workflow.
Does the overlay block my mouse or keyboard?
No! With click-through mode enabled, the overlay is completely invisible to your mouse. You can code, browse, and click through it as if it isn't there. Toggle it on/off anytime from the dashboard.
Does it work without an internet connection?
Yes. HabitGlo works fully offline using local storage. Cloud sync is optional — sign in to sync habits, streaks, and settings across devices.
Is my data private on the leaderboard?
Absolutely. Your habits are private by default and never appear on the global leaderboard unless you explicitly opt in with the "Share my habits" checkbox. You control what's visible.
What platforms are supported?
Currently Windows 10+ as a native desktop app. Mac and Linux support is planned. You can also try HabitGlo in your browser — add habits, pick themes, and preview the scrolling overlay without installing anything.
Can I cancel my monthly subscription?
Yes, cancel anytime. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period. Your habits and streak data are never deleted.
