# HabitGlo: how it works

HabitGlo scrolls your reminders across a thin LED strip at the top of your screen, all day.

## First launch

The LED strip appears docked at the top of your main monitor with five starter reminders. The HabitGlo window opens next to it. No account or internet connection is needed to start.

## The window

From top to bottom:

- **LED preview.** Shows exactly what the strip shows.
  - **Start / Pause** freezes or resumes the scrolling.
  - **Show LED / Hide LED** puts the strip on screen or takes it away.
- **Add a reminder.** Type and press **Enter** (220 characters max; any language works).
- **Your reminders.**
  - Drag the handle on the left to change the order.
  - Use the switch to take a reminder off the strip without deleting it.
  - Use the bin to delete it.
  - Click a reminder's text to edit it: **Enter** saves, **Esc** cancels.

## Settings (gear icon)

- **Speed:** Slow, Medium or Fast.
- **Position:** Top or Bottom of your main monitor.
  - **Keep windows from covering it** reserves the strip's space like a second taskbar, so maximised windows stop at its edge.
- **Color:** Classic LED (red, amber, green), Neon (pink, blue), Calm blue, or Mono white.
- **Account:** your email and plan, Manage billing, Sign out, or sign in.

## Tray icon

- **Left-click** the HabitGlo icon in the Windows tray to open the window.
- **Right-click** for **Open HabitGlo**, **Show LED / Hide LED**, and **Quit**.
- Closing the window only hides it; the strip keeps running. Use **Quit** to stop HabitGlo completely and give the screen space back.

## Offline and accounts

- Your reminders are saved on your PC first, so adding, editing and reordering work without internet. If you are signed in, changes sync to your account in the background when you are back online.
- A paid plan is checked online. If you are offline, HabitGlo keeps working for **14 days** after the last successful check. After that it asks you to connect once.
- Plans and pricing: [habitglo.com/pricing](https://habitglo.com/pricing).

## Keyboard

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Add a reminder | Type, then **Enter** |
| Edit / cancel edit | Click the text, then **Enter** / **Esc** |
| Reorder | **Tab** to the handle, **Space**, arrow keys, **Space** |
| Close settings | **Esc** |
